Project 01

Natural contemporary home

A contemporary residential project blending natural materials, warm neutrals, and soft textures to create a calm, elegant, and timeless atmosphere connected to nature.

01.1

Entrance

A warm, contemporary interior combining natural wood, textured plaster, fluted detailing, and soft stone finishes. Earthy neutral tones and warm ambient lighting create a calm, intimate, and sophisticated atmosphere, while sculptural décor and layered textures add depth and character to the space.

Mood board
01.2

Living room

A serene living room continuing the same warm, natural palette, with soft textured fabrics, natural wood, and subtle stone details creating a relaxed and inviting feel. Surrounded by lush greenery and overlooking the swimming pool, the space blends indoor comfort with nature to create a calm, refreshing retreat.

Armchair Bouclé upholstery, walnut frame
01.3

Kitchen

A warm and sophisticated kitchen designed with natural wood cabinetry, stone surfaces, textured plaster, and subtle dark accents. The wooden stools add a handcrafted touch, while soft integrated lighting enhances the rich materials and creates a calm, welcoming, and timeless atmosphere.

Mood board
Study area

The layout maximizes functionality, with a compact study area thoughtfully integrated between the entrance and living room, creating a practical yet seamless transition.

01.4

Guest area

The guest area combines modern elegance with subtle Arabic influences, featuring a refined wall artwork and a simple wooden TV unit framed between two windows. Warm materials and clean lines create a sophisticated, masculine atmosphere that feels both welcoming and timeless.

Project 02

Living room

A simple and elegant living room designed with clean painted walls, minimal artwork, and a sleek wall-mounted TV unit. The dining area is naturally connected to the space, while an L-shaped corner window brings in abundant daylight and frames a beautiful view of the surrounding greenery. A simple marble staircase adds a refined touch while keeping the overall design calm and uncluttered.

Project 03

Entrance

The entrance is designed around strong vertical proportions and a balanced architectural composition. An oversized timber door with subtle brass detailing creates a refined focal point, while the U-shaped staircase frames the double-height glazed opening and maximizes natural light. A contemporary marble console anchors one side, balanced by a full-height storage element on the other, with integrated greenery softening the architectural lines.

Project 01 · Production company

35 Film

The client provided a selection of reference designs that reflected his preferred style and aesthetic. These references were carefully studied and used as inspiration, allowing us to develop a design concept that reflects his vision while creating a cohesive and personalized interior.

Offices

Manager's room & meeting area

A modern manager's office designed for a dynamic production company, combining a refined executive workspace with a comfortable meeting area. Clean architectural lines, contemporary furniture, and a restrained material palette create a professional yet creative atmosphere. The meeting zone encourages collaboration, while the manager's desk maintains a strong, elegant presence without feeling overly formal.

Staff room & meeting area

Lobby

The lobby is designed as a vibrant social hub for 35 Film, featuring a compact stage and gathering area alongside a kitchenette and coffee station. The company logo creates a strong focal point, while the interior palette draws directly from the brand's orange and green tones, balanced with warm beige and natural greenery. The result is a creative, welcoming space that reflects the company's energetic identity.

Project 03 · Factory design

Fine silver jewelry factory

A luxury jewelry space should feel just as valuable as the pieces it showcases.

For a fine silver jewelry factory, the design focused on creating a timeless luxury experience through warm neutral tones, rich dark finishes, bronze detailing, marble elements, and soft layered lighting. The result is a sophisticated space where elegance, warmth, and brand identity come together to elevate the customer experience.

Project 04

Restaurant

Designing for experience, not just aesthetics ..

This restaurant concept explores how lighting, materiality, and spatial layering can influence guest perception and behavior. Warm tones, tactile finishes, and controlled lighting create an environment that encourages longer stays and a more immersive dining experience. In hospitality design, success isn't only about visual impact — it's about how a space performs emotionally and commercially.
